How DharmaDecision-AI Works

A lightweight, explainable retrieval pipeline connects a user's question with candidate ethical principles in the available corpus.

01

Ethical Dilemma

The user enters a real-world ethical question or situation.

02

Text Preprocessing

The question is tokenized and expanded with related ethical concepts to help match different ways of describing a situation.

03

Lightweight Evidence Retrieval

Keyword-overlap scoring and concept expansion rank records from the local Vidura Niti knowledge base. This version does not use semantic embeddings or FAISS.

04

Evidence and Relevance

Up to three candidate principles are displayed with their stored source metadata and a relevance indicator. Relevance is not proof of correctness.

05

Explainable Decision Support

The system offers a cautious recommendation grounded in retrieved paraphrases and applies a separate rule-based safety response to certain potentially violent requests.

06

Limitations and Feedback

The current corpus is small. Feedback, where successfully saved, is collected for future evaluation and does not automatically retrain the retrieval system.

About DharmaDecision-AI

Ancient ethical principles. Modern decision support.

This academic prototype explores how principles paraphrased from Vidura Niti can be retrieved and presented as perspectives for contemporary ethical dilemmas. Its output should be interpreted critically, and source passages should be verified against reliable editions before formal academic citation.
